About

What is P's Card Play?

P's Card Play is a free, browser-based card game hub featuring 10 classic games — no downloads, no accounts, no paywalls. Just pick a game and play.

The Games

We offer Memory Match, War, Texas Hold'em, Solitaire, Blackjack, Go Fish, Baccarat, Crazy Eights, Gin Rummy, and 5-Card Draw. Every game runs entirely in your browser and works on desktop and mobile.

Leaderboards

Scores are saved locally on your device. Enter your name on the home page to track your best performances across all 10 games and compete with yourself over time.

Privacy

We don't collect any personal data. All scores and your name are stored only on your device using your browser's local storage. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
