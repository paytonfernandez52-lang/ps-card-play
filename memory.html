🃏 How to Play

Goal

🎯Find all 8 matching pairs of cards before running out of patience!

How to Play

1.16 cards are placed face down in a 4×4 grid.
2.Click any card to flip it over and reveal the image.
3.Click a second card to try and find its match.
4.If the two cards match, they stay face up with a gold glow.
5.If they don't match, both cards flip back over after a moment.
6.Find all 8 pairs to win!

Scoring

📊Your score is the total number of moves (each pair of flips = 1 move).
🏆Fewer moves = better score. Lower scores rank higher on the leaderboard.

Tips

💡Remember where each card is — the whole game is about memory!
🔀Cards are reshuffled randomly every new game.
