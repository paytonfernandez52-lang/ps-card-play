Crazy Eights — How to Play

Goal: Be the first to empty your hand.

Playing a card: You may play a card that matches the top discard's suit or rank.

Eights are wild: An 8 can be played at any time. After playing an 8, choose the new suit for the next player to match.

Drawing: If you have no playable card, draw from the deck. You may draw as many times as needed until you get something playable or the deck runs out.

Empty deck: When the deck is empty and you can't play, your turn passes.

Winning: Empty your hand to win! Your score for the leaderboard is your cumulative win count.

CPU strategy: The CPU prefers to match rank, then suit, plays eights as a last resort, and picks the suit it holds the most of.
