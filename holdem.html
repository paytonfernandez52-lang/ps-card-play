♠ How to Play

Goal

🎯Win chips by making the best 5-card poker hand, or by getting CPU to fold.

The Deal

1.Each player gets 2 private hole cards.
2.5 community cards are revealed across 3 stages: Flop (3 cards), Turn (+1), River (+1).
3.Make the best 5-card hand from any combination of your 2 + the 5 community cards.

Betting Rounds

🪙Blinds — Small blind $5 and big blind $10 are posted automatically before the deal.
🃏Pre-Flop → Flop → Turn → River — bet each round.

Actions

🚫Fold — Give up your hand. CPU wins the pot.
✅Check — Pass (only when no bet to call).
📞Call — Match the current bet.
⬆️Bet / Raise — Increase the stakes. Pick ½ Pot, Pot, or All-in.

Hand Rankings (Best → Worst)

👑Royal Flush · Straight Flush · Four of a Kind · Full House · Flush
🃏Straight · Three of a Kind · Two Pair · One Pair · High Card
