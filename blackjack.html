🃏 How to Play

Goal

🎯Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over (busting).

Card Values

🔢Number cards (2–10) = face value.
👑Jack, Queen, King = 10.
🂡Ace = 11, or 1 if 11 would bust you.

Betting

🪙Click chip buttons to build your bet, then press Deal.
💰Win = bet paid 1:1. Blackjack (Ace + 10-value on first deal) = paid 3:2.
🤝Tie (push) = your bet is returned.

Actions

✅Hit — Draw another card.
🛑Stand — Keep your hand; dealer plays.
✌️Double — Double your bet, take exactly one more card, then stand.

Dealer Rules

📋Dealer must hit on 16 or less, stand on 17 or more.
🃏Dealer's second card is hidden until you stand.
