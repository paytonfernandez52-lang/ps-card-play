🐟 How to Play

Goal

🎯Collect the most books (all 4 cards of a rank, e.g. all four 7s) to win.

Your Turn

1.Choose a rank from your hand to ask the CPU for.
2.If the CPU has any cards of that rank, they hand them all over — and you go again!
3.If the CPU has none — Go Fish! You draw from the deck.
4.If the drawn card matches what you asked for, you go again. Otherwise it's the CPU's turn.

Books

📚When you collect all 4 cards of a rank, it forms a book and is set aside automatically.

Winning

🏆The game ends when all 13 books are collected. Most books wins!
🃏If the deck runs out and a player has no cards, they can't play further.
