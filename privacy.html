Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Overview

P's Card Play ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our service:

  • Your name — entered voluntarily on the home page, stored only in your browser's local storage. Never transmitted to any server.
  • Game scores — saved locally on your device to display leaderboards. Never transmitted to any server.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment information, or any personally identifiable information.

Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to save your name and scores. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Third-Party Services

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

We use Tailwind CSS loaded from a CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com). No personal data is shared with this service.

Children's Privacy

P's Card Play does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The site is intended for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
