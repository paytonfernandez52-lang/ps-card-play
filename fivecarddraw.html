5-Card Draw — How to Play

Goal: Make the best 5-card poker hand and win the pot.

Blinds: You post the small blind, CPU posts the big blind (or vice versa based on dealer button).

Pre-draw betting: After cards are dealt, a round of betting takes place.

The Draw: Click cards to toggle Hold/Discard, then press Draw to replace the discards (up to 5).

Post-draw betting: A final betting round before showdown.

Showdown: Best hand wins the pot. Hands ranked (highest to lowest): Royal Flush, Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Flush, Straight, Three of a Kind, Two Pair, One Pair, High Card.

Starting chips: $500. Leaderboard records your highest chip total.
