Gin Rummy — How to Play

Goal: Form your 10-card hand into melds (sets/runs) and minimize deadwood (unmelded cards).

Melds: A set is 3–4 cards of the same rank. A run is 3+ consecutive cards in the same suit (e.g. 4♠ 5♠ 6♠). Ace is always low (A-2-3).

Deadwood points: Ace = 1, numbered cards = face value, J/Q/K = 10.

Turn: Draw from the deck or pick up the discard pile's top card. Then discard one card.

Knocking: When your deadwood is ≤ 10 points, you may knock after drawing. Click a card to discard it, then press Knock.

Gin: If your deadwood reaches 0, you can declare Gin for a 25-point bonus.

Showdown: After you knock, the CPU lays off its cards onto your melds if possible. If the CPU's deadwood ≤ yours, it wins with an undercut bonus (+25). Otherwise you score the difference.

Winning: First to 100 points wins the match. Score is your cumulative match wins.
