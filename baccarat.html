🎰 How to Play

Goal

🎯Bet on whether the Player hand or Banker hand will be closer to 9 — or if they'll tie.

Card Values

🔢Ace = 1  ·  2–9 = face value  ·  10, J, Q, K = 0
➕Hand total = sum of cards mod 10 (so a 7 and 8 = 15 → value is 5).

Betting

👤Player — pays 1:1 if Player hand wins.
🏦Banker — pays 0.95:1 (5% commission) if Banker wins. Slightly better odds.
🤝Tie — pays 8:1 if both hands equal. All bets on Player/Banker are returned on a tie.

Drawing Rules

⭐Natural: If either hand totals 8 or 9 on the first 2 cards — no more cards drawn.
👤Player draws a 3rd card on total 0–5, stands on 6–7.
🏦Banker follows specific rules based on their total and whether Player drew — handled automatically.
