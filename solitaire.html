🂡 How to Play

Goal

🎯Move all 52 cards to the 4 foundation piles, sorted by suit from Ace up to King.

Setup

📋7 tableau columns — column 1 has 1 card, column 2 has 2, etc. Only the top card faces up.
🃏Remaining cards form the Stock (top left). Click it to flip a card to the Waste pile.

Moving Cards

1.Click a card to select it (gold highlight), then click the destination.
🏗️Tableau: Stack cards in alternating colors, descending rank (e.g. red 6 on black 7).
👑Only a King can be placed on an empty tableau column.
📦Foundations: Build each suit from Ace → King. Click a card then click the matching foundation.
🔄You can move stacks of face-up cards together in the tableau.

Stock

🔄Click to flip 1 card to Waste. When empty, click to recycle all Waste cards back.
🏆Win by filling all 4 foundations. Fewer moves = better leaderboard score!
🎉

You Win!

All cards sent to foundations!
