⚔️ How to Play

Goal

🎯Capture all 52 cards to win. The player who runs out of cards loses.

How to Play

1.You and the CPU each start with 26 cards (half the deck).
2.Press Draw Cards — both players flip their top card.
3.The higher card wins and takes both cards, adding them to the bottom of their pile.
4.Keep drawing until one player has all 52 cards.

Card Values (Low → High)

🃏2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6 · 7 · 8 · 9 · 10 · Jack · Queen · King · Ace
👑Ace is the highest card in the game.

WAR! (Tie)

⚔️When both cards have equal value, it's WAR!
1.Each player places 3 cards face down into the pot.
2.Then each player flips 1 final battle card face up.
3.The higher battle card wins the entire pot.
🤝If the battle cards also tie, the pot is split evenly.

Leaderboard

🏆Win a full game to add a win to your leaderboard count.
📈Your wins stack up — the more you win, the higher you rank!
